We calculate the following metrics:

  • Precision: The ratio of correctly detected objects to the total number of detected objects, measuring the accuracy of positive predictions.
  • Recall: The ratio of correctly detected objects to the total number of actual objects, assessing the model's ability to find all relevant instances.
  • 3D IoU (Intersection over Union): The ratio of the intersection volume to the union volume between a predicted and a ground truth 3D bounding box, measuring spatial overlap.
  • Position RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): The square root of the mean squared differences between predicted and ground truth positions, evaluating localization accuracy.
  • Rotation RMSE: The square root of the mean squared differences between predicted and ground truth rotations, quantifying angular estimation errors.
  • 3D mAP (Mean Average Precision): The average precision across different intersection-over-union (IoU) thresholds in 3D object detection, reflecting overall detection performance.
